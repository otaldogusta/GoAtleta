## Prompt — recepção

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: single illustrative example photo inside a fictional youth volleyball class report UI for Go Atleta. Create one natural candid smartphone photograph, landscape 4:3. Scene: a modest Brazilian indoor community sports gym, blue and warm terracotta court floor, volleyball net in the background, afternoon daylight through high windows. Six fictional teenage students in plain athletic T-shirts and shorts, sneakers, practicing volleyball reception in pairs with a coach at the side. Documentary wide shot from the sideline, natural unstaged postures, one player doing a forearm pass, believable spacing, realistic ball and net, faces small and incidental. Neutral natural colors, realistic skin and anatomy, no dramatic cinematic lighting, no poster design. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no collage. Entirely synthetic people and setting.

## Prompt — orientação

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one illustrative photo for a fictional Go Atleta youth volleyball class report. Single natural candid smartphone photograph, landscape 4:3. A modest Brazilian indoor sports gym with a blue and terracotta court and pale concrete walls. Six fictional teenage students in plain navy and white sports T-shirts, shorts and sneakers have gathered in a loose small semicircle on the court, listening to an adult coach holding a blue-yellow volleyball during a practice break. Shot diagonally from the sideline at eye level, subjects fully in frame at medium distance. Volleyball net visible behind them. Natural afternoon light, realistic everyday colors, unposed documentary atmosphere, realistic anatomy. No text, logos, watermark, graphic design, UI or collage. Entirely fictional synthetic people. The result should look like an ordinary useful photo documenting a class, not an advertising shoot.
